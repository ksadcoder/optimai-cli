# Capacity Planning and Sizing Guide

Guidelines for VPS resource allocation when deploying OptimAI nodes.

| Metric | Minimum | Recommended | Production Fleet |
|---|---|---|---|
| CPU | 1 vCPU | 2 vCPU | 4 vCPU |
| RAM | 1 GB (+2G swap) | 2 GB | 4 GB+ |
| Disk | 15 GB SSD | 25 GB NVMe | 50 GB NVMe |
| Bandwidth | 500 GB/mo | 1 TB/mo | Unlimited |
